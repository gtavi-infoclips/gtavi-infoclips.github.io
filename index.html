FLIPA Publicador

FLIPA Publicador is the internal publishing tool of FLIPA, a Spanish-language content channel about video games (GTA 6 clips, news and curiosities). It lets the channel owner upload the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account, choosing the title, privacy level and interaction settings before each post.

How it works

  1. The owner connects the FLIPA TikTok account with Login Kit (TikTok authorization).
  2. The owner selects one of the channel's own finished videos, writes the caption and chooses privacy and interaction options.
  3. After explicit confirmation, the video is sent with the Content Posting API (Direct Post). Nothing is posted without the owner's action.
