Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

FLIPA Publicador is a private tool used only by the owners of FLIPA. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not collect data about visitors.

Data we access

When the owner connects the FLIPA TikTok account, we receive the account's public profile (display name, avatar) and the permission to post videos on its behalf. Access tokens are stored privately in the owner's Google account (Google Apps Script properties) and are never shared.

Use of data

Data is used only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. We do not sell, share or use data for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the connection is active. The owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) or by emailing gtavi.infoclips@gmail.com, and we will delete the stored data.
